Fig. 01 — About

Grounded in the microstructure, built for the application

I'm a materials scientist with a PhD and specialized expertise in advanced materials for aerospace applications, currently working as a Scientific Specialist for the Materials and Special Processes team at Liebherr Aerospace Toulouse, through Capgemini Engineering.

My track record spans R&D, process optimization, and materials characterization within a tier-1 aerospace supply chain. Before that, my PhD work at I2M (CNRS) took a deep dive into hydrogen embrittlement in laser powder-bed-fusion stainless steels — coupling electrochemistry, mechanical testing, and microstructural analysis across a three-lab collaboration.

I like problems that sit at the intersection of process and property: why a joint fails, why a microstructure embrittles, why a fin geometry doesn't form the way the drawing says it should — and then finding the fix that actually ships.

Fig. 03 — Experience

Work experience

Click a role to expand the details.

  • Compact aluminium heat exchangers (Next Single Aisle): solved a brazing-joint quality issue on 80 µm fins by tracing clad diffusion through extensive microstructural analysis — findings that led to a change in Liebherr's fin-forming process.
  • Graphite seal rings for valves: built an ASTM-compliant material characterization framework and supplier qualification protocol, giving the team an end-to-end path to evaluate and select vendors.
  • Inconel 718 valve body redesign: led microstructural characterization across casting, forging, and additive manufacturing routes, linking microstructure to post-heat-treatment properties to guide material selection for high-temperature service.
  • Made the transition from academic research to corporate R&D — adapting quickly to industry workflows and cross-functional collaboration to turn material insight into implemented solutions.
  • Investigated hydrogen embrittlement in laser powder-bed-fusion 316L, 304L, and 17-4PH stainless steels under tensile and high-cycle fatigue loading.
  • Designed and built a coupled fatigue–hydrogen electrochemical testing rig, running high-pressure hydrogen gas mechanical tests as part of a three-lab collaboration.
  • Managed procurement of powders and lab equipment end-to-end — supplier communication, quoting, and coordination — to keep the lab running.
  • Fabricated, machined, and characterized specimens, communicated results regularly, and mentored Master's students and interns on the project.
