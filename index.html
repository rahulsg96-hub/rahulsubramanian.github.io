§ 01 — About

Grounded in the microstructure, built for the application

Languages

EnglishNative / Bilingual
FrenchFull Professional

I'm a structural modelling and simulation engineer at Design Tech Centre in Paris, where I design and analyse components for small, innovative aircraft powered by new energy sources — backed by a PhD in materials science and a background in aerospace materials R&D.

Before moving into simulation, my work was hands-on: a PhD at I2M (CNRS) on hydrogen embrittlement in laser powder-bed-fusion stainless steels — coupling electrochemistry, mechanical testing, and microstructural analysis across a three-lab collaboration — followed by R&D, process optimization, and materials characterization within a tier-1 aerospace supply chain. That background shapes how I build, and question, a model.

I like problems that sit at the intersection of process and property: why a joint fails, why a microstructure embrittles, why a fin geometry doesn't form the way the drawing says it should — and then finding the fix that actually ships.

I also build with AI. I'm automating my FEA pipeline with Python and Claude Code, and this portfolio — including the live wing-rib analysis above — was built the same way.
